## Long Description

SNAP Life is the first app of its kind dedicated to helping people understand, manage and improve their bone health while supporting healthy ageing, mobility, wellbeing and longevity.

Whether you are living with osteoporosis, osteopenia, recovering from a fracture, navigating menopause, concerned about future bone loss, or simply want to build stronger bones for life, SNAP Life provides personalised support every step of the way.

Designed for both women and men, SNAP Life combines expert-led education, intelligent technology, wellness support and practical tracking tools to help you take control of your bone health and future wellbeing.

Why SNAP Life?

Bone health impacts every stage of life. Strong bones support mobility, independence, confidence and quality of life as we age. SNAP Life brings together everything you need to better understand, monitor and support your bone health in one easy-to-use platform.

Key features:

- Personalised bone health guidance
- Bone Buddy AI wellness companion
- Osteoporosis and osteopenia education
- Expert-led learning pathways
- Guided wellness and relaxation sessions
- DEXA and FRAX tracking
- Supplement and medication tracking
- Nutrition and healthy habit support
- Menopause and hormonal health education
- Progress tracking and motivation
- Access to expert support and coaching

Learn through engaging pathways covering osteoporosis, osteopenia, bone health fundamentals, nutrition, supplementation, strength, balance, movement, menopause, hormonal health, stress, sleep, recovery, confidence, mindset, lifestyle habits, healthy ageing and longevity.

Track DEXA scan results, FRAX fracture risk assessments, supplements, medications, nutrition, daily wellbeing habits and bone health progress.

Meet Bone Buddy, your personalised bone health and wellbeing companion. Bone Buddy helps you better understand your bone health, stay motivated between appointments, build healthier habits, learn at your own pace and feel supported throughout your journey.

Enjoy guided wellness experiences for calm, focus, energy, sleep support, stress relief, confidence, mindset, happiness and emotional balance.

SNAP Life helps people living with osteoporosis, living with osteopenia, concerned about fracture risk, supporting a loved one, navigating menopause, improving mobility and strength, focused on prevention, or committed to healthy ageing and longevity.

Important: SNAP Life provides educational, wellness and lifestyle support only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical concerns, medications and treatment decisions.
