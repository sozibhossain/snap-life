Enhance the “Breathing Studio” within SNAP Life to deliver a premium, state-driven experience designed to actively shift how users feel.

This system should guide users through different emotional and physical states using breath, sound, and visual design.

---

CORE PRINCIPLE:

Each session must be designed to influence a specific state:
CALM → STRESS RELIEF → FOCUS → ENERGY → SLEEP

Each state must feel distinct in rhythm, sound, and outcome.

---

SESSION TYPES:

1. CALM (Nervous System Reset):
Goal: Reduce general tension and create a sense of calm

- Breathing:
  4s inhale / 6–8s exhale

- Music:
  - Soft ambient tones
  - Warm, low-frequency sound
  - Light nature elements

- Effect:
  Letting go, slowing down

---

2. STRESS (Active Stress Relief):
Goal: Quickly reduce stress and overwhelm

- Breathing:
  Slightly deeper breathing with extended exhale
  (e.g. 4s inhale / 6s exhale)

- Music:
  - Gentle but slightly more guided than Calm
  - Subtle rhythmic grounding tones
  - Reassuring, steady sound

- Effect:
  Moving from “overwhelmed” → “in control”

---

3. FOCUS (Clarity & Balance):
Goal: Improve mental clarity and attention

- Breathing:
  Balanced box breathing (4-4-4-4)

- Music:
  - Clean, light ambient tones
  - Subtle rhythm
  - Slightly brighter frequencies

- Effect:
  Clear, sharp, present

---

4. ENERGY (Uplift & Activation):
Goal: Increase alertness and motivation

- Breathing:
  Slightly faster rhythm (e.g. 3s inhale / 3s exhale)

- Music:
  - Light, uplifting ambient tones
  - Gentle rhythmic pulse
  - Brighter, higher-frequency sounds (not harsh)

- Effect:
  Awake, motivated, ready

---

5. SLEEP (Deep Relaxation):
Goal: Prepare body for sleep

- Breathing:
  Very slow (e.g. 4s inhale / 8s exhale)

- Music:
  - Deep ambient sound
  - Low-frequency tones
  - Soft continuous textures (tranquil, almost hypnotic)
  - Optional subtle night ambience

- Effect:
  Heavy, relaxed, drifting into sleep

---

SESSION DURATIONS:

Each session offers 3 options:

1. RESET (Short):
- 2–3 minutes
- Quick emotional shift

2. RECONNECT (Medium):
- 5–7 minutes
- Mindfulness + body awareness

3. DEEP (Long):
- 10–15 minutes
- Nervous system regulation and deeper impact

---

VISUAL EXPERIENCE:

- Breathing orb animation:
  - Expand (inhale)
  - Hold (pause)
  - Contract (exhale)

- Smooth transitions
- Subtle glow

- Colour themes:
  - Calm → soft green/blue
  - Stress → warm neutral tones
  - Focus → light blue
  - Energy → soft yellow/orange (gentle, not bright)
  - Sleep → deep blue/purple

---

AUDIO EXPERIENCE:

- Music must:
  - Match session state precisely
  - Fade in/out smoothly
  - Loop seamlessly

- Controls:
  - Sound ON/OFF
  - Volume slider

- Ensure:
  - No harsh sounds
  - No sudden changes
  - Emotionally aligned tone

---

OPTIONAL GUIDANCE:

- Light voice prompts:
  “Inhale… Exhale…”

- Calm, slow tone
- Toggle ON/OFF

---

HAPTIC FEEDBACK:

- Gentle vibration:
  - Start inhale
  - Start exhale

- Optional toggle

---

SESSION COMPLETION (SNAP SIGNATURE):

At the end:

Display:
“Pause. Notice how you feel.”

Then:
“How do you feel now?”

Options:
- Calm
- Clear
- Relaxed
- Energised
- Still tense

Store response in user log.

Reinforce:
“Small daily moments build a stronger body and calmer mind.”

---

PERFORMANCE:

- Preload audio
- Smooth animation
- Mobile optimised
- Reliable on slower connections

GOAL:

Create a breathing system that adapts to real-life emotional needs.

Users should be able to choose how they feel — and leave each session feeling noticeably different.

This should become a daily SNAP Life ritual.
