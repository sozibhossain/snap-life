Conduct a full UI/UX refinement of SNAP Life to ensure the app is not only clean and functional, but also visually attractive, emotionally engaging, and premium in feel.

This is a final polish to elevate the app beyond standard wellbeing tools.

---

CORE OBJECTIVE:

- Create a visually attractive, premium experience
- Maintain simplicity and clarity
- Reinforce SNAP Life as a lifestyle brand, not just a health app

---

1. VISUAL ATTRACTIVENESS (KEY PRIORITY):

Enhance the overall look and feel:

- Use soft gradients instead of flat colours
- Introduce subtle depth (cards, shadows, layering)
- Ensure smooth transitions and animations
- Add gentle motion where appropriate (micro-interactions)

The app should feel:
- Modern
- Calm
- Premium
- Inviting

---

2. COLOUR SYSTEM:

Refine colour palette:

- Calm tones (soft blues, greens, warm neutrals)
- Use accent colours sparingly for key actions
- Differentiate sections subtly (e.g. breathing vs nutrition)

Avoid:
- Harsh contrasts
- Overly bright or clinical colours

---

3. TYPOGRAPHY:

- Use clean, modern fonts
- Create hierarchy:
  - Headings (clear and calm)
  - Body text (easy to read)
- Ensure spacing improves readability

---

4. MICRO-INTERACTIONS:

Add small but meaningful animations:

- Button presses (soft feedback)
- Breathing orb transitions
- Page transitions (smooth fade/slide)

Keep all motion:
- Slow
- Smooth
- Non-distracting

---

5. EMOTIONAL DESIGN:

Each screen should evoke a feeling:

- Breathing Studio → calm, immersive
- Nutrition → simple, supportive
- Home → clarity and direction
- SNAP Shot → rewarding and positive

---

6. HOME SCREEN POLISH:

- Clean layout with clear hierarchy
- Highlight:
  - “Today’s Focus”
  - Bone Buddy message
- Use spacing to avoid clutter
- Add subtle visual cues (icons, cards)

---

7. CARD-BASED DESIGN:

- Use soft rounded cards for content
- Add light shadow or elevation
- Keep spacing consistent

This creates:
- Structure
- Visual comfort
- Premium feel

---

8. ICONOGRAPHY:

- Use consistent, simple icons
- Avoid overly complex visuals
- Align icon style across all features

---

9. BRAND PERSONALITY (SNAP LIFE):

The app should feel:

- Supportive, not clinical
- Stylish, not generic
- Calm, not boring

Tone should reflect:
- Wellness + lifestyle + intelligence

---

10. REMOVE VISUAL CLUTTER:

- Eliminate unnecessary text
- Avoid crowded screens
- Keep whitespace intentional

Less = more.

---

11. LOADING & TRANSITIONS:

- Use smooth fade-ins
- Avoid abrupt changes
- Keep experience fluid

---

12. PREMIUM FINISH:

Add subtle touches that elevate perception:

- Soft background gradients
- Slight blur effects where appropriate
- Refined spacing and alignment

The app should feel like:
a high-end wellness product.

---

13. CONSISTENCY CHECK:

Ensure all screens:

- Follow same design rules
- Use same spacing system
- Align visually and structurally

---

GOAL:

Deliver an app that is:

- Beautiful
- Calm
- Easy to use
- Emotionally engaging

Users should not only find it useful — they should enjoy using it and feel good every time they open it.

This should feel like a premium lifestyle experience, not just a functional tool.
