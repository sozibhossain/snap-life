## MODULE STRUCTURE (DUOLINGO-STYLE MICRO LEARNING)

Break into short progressive lessons:

---

## LESSON 1: WHY TRACKING MATTERS

Explain:

* bone health is influenced by daily consistency
* supplements and medications support prevention and treatment
* tracking helps users build awareness and routine confidence

Key message:
“You don’t need perfection — just consistency.”

---

## LESSON 2: SUPPLEMENTS VS MEDICATIONS (SIMPLE CLARITY)

Explain clearly:

* Supplements = nutritional support (vitamin D, calcium, magnesium, etc.)
* Medications = prescribed clinical treatments (e.g. bisphosphonates, HRT, denosumab)

Important:

* Both are part of bone health care
* Both can be logged in SNAP
* Always follow clinician guidance for medications

---

## LESSON 3: UNDERSTANDING DOSAGE (CRITICAL UX EDUCATION)

Teach:

* mg = milligrams
* mcg = micrograms
* IU = international units
* g = grams

Explain:

* different substances use different units
* prescriptions must be followed exactly
* SNAP helps users record, not interpret clinical dosing

---

## LESSON 4: HOW TO LOG IN SNAP (MOST IMPORTANT STEP)

This is the core behavioural bridge.

Show users EXACTLY how to use the existing system:

STEP 1:
Go to “Nutrition / Intake Tracker” (existing feature)

STEP 2:
Select:

* Supplement OR Medication

STEP 3:
Search or add item

STEP 4:
Enter:

* dose
* unit (mg, mcg, IU, etc.)
* frequency
* timing (optional)

STEP 5:
Save to “Today’s Intake”

STEP 6:
Mark as taken (optional daily check-in)

---

## LESSON 5: HOW THIS LINKS TO YOUR BONE HEALTH TRACKER

Explain simply:

* your intake contributes to your Bone Health insights
* consistency helps build long-term strength habits
* SNAP tracks behaviour patterns (not medical outcomes)

Avoid clinical claims.

Use language like:

* “helps you understand your routine”
* “builds awareness of your daily habits”
* “supports your healthy ageing journey”

---

## LESSON 6: BUILDING YOUR DAILY ROUTINE

Encourage habit formation:

* take supplements consistently
* log medications simply
* don’t aim for perfection
* aim for routine stability

Introduce:

* gentle streaks (optional)
* daily check-in habit
* “Today’s Intake” view
