Enhance the SNAP Life subscription system using RevenueCat to support fully live Plus and Premium tiers, with clear, flexible pricing and a premium user experience.

SUBSCRIPTION STRUCTURE:

1. Free Trial:
- Duration: 7 days
- Full access to ALL features (Premium level)

IMPORTANT:
- After Day 3, prompt user to add credit card details:
  “To ensure uninterrupted access after your trial”

- Do NOT charge until trial ends
- Ensure seamless continuation with no disruption

---

2. SNAP PLUS:

- Price: £9.99/month
- Cancel anytime

PLUS includes:
- Full Breathing Studio (all sessions)
- Expanded Meditation Library (limited vs Premium)
- Daily Check-In (mood logging)
- Basic mood history (recent entries only)
- Core Meal Plans (non-advanced personalisation)
- Bone Buddy (standard guidance)

---

3. SNAP PREMIUM:

- Price: £19.99/month
- Cancel anytime

- Annual Plan:
  - 20% discount
  - Paid upfront
  - Also “Cancel anytime” (no renewal obligation)

PREMIUM INCLUDES EVERYTHING IN PLUS, PLUS:
