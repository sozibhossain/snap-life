Build a personalised Meal Plan and Nutrition section within SNAP Life that removes decision-making for the user and supports daily eating habits for bone health.

The system must be simple, guided, and tailored using user data (FRAX score, bone health assessment, and risk profile).

The goal is to help users consistently meet their nutritional needs without having to think about food combinations.

---

CORE PRINCIPLE:

Deliver a “done-for-you” daily nutrition experience that:
- Is personalised
- Is easy to follow
- Supports bone health, joint health, and overall wellbeing

---

PERSONALISATION ENGINE:

Use the following inputs:
- FRAX score (fracture risk)
- Bone health assessment results
- Age
- Gender
- Lifestyle factors (if available)

Based on this, generate:
- Daily nutritional targets:
  - Calcium
  - Vitamin D
  - Protein
  - Magnesium (optional)
- Suggested supplement support (if needed)

---

MEAL PLAN STRUCTURE:

Provide a daily plan including:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner
- Optional snacks

Each day should:
- Meet nutritional targets
- Be balanced and realistic
- Avoid complexity

---

RECIPES (KEY FEATURE):

Each meal must include:

1. Simple Recipe:
- Ingredients list (clear + minimal)
- Step-by-step instructions
- Max 5–7 steps

2. Quick Prep Indicator:
- Time required (e.g. “10 mins”, “20 mins”)

3. Nutritional Highlight:
- Example:
  “High in calcium and protein”

---

“HOW TO” GUIDES (IMPORTANT):

Include a section for simple guidance:

- “How to build a bone-friendly meal”
- “Easy ways to increase calcium daily”
- “Simple swaps for better nutrition”

Keep these:
- Short
- Practical
- Easy to follow

---

USER EXPERIENCE:

- Daily plan view (today’s meals)
- Option to:
  - Swap meals
  - Save favourites
- Keep interface clean and visual

Avoid:
- Overwhelming data
- Complex nutritional breakdowns

---

SUPPLEMENTS GUIDANCE:

- Suggest supplements where needed:
  Example:
  “Based on your profile, consider Vitamin D support”

- Keep guidance general (not medical advice)
- Position as support, not requirement

---

ADAPTABILITY:

- Allow users to:
  - Regenerate meal plans
  - Select preferences (if available later)
    - Vegetarian
    - Dairy-free

---

INTEGRATION WITH SNAP SYSTEM:

- Reinforce messaging:
  “Consistent nutrition supports stronger bones over time”

- Align with overall SNAP Life approach:
  - Simple
  - Daily habits
  - Long-term health

---

FUTURE-READY (DO NOT FULLY BUILD YET):

- Personalised weekly plans
- Shopping list generation
- Deeper nutritional tracking

---

GOAL:

Create a personalised, easy-to-follow meal system that removes guesswork and helps users build consistent, bone-supporting eating habits.

The experience should feel supportive, practical, and achievable — not clinical or overwhelming.
