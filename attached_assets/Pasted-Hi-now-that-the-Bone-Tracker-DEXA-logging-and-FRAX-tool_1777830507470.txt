Hi — now that the Bone Tracker, DEXA logging, and FRAX tool are being enhanced, we need to elevate the dashboard experience to a high-end, premium standard.

This should not feel like a medical dashboard or basic charts — it should feel like a luxury wellness product that is calm, intelligent, and visually engaging.

Please implement the following:

---

SNAP LIFE – PREMIUM DASHBOARD EXPERIENCE (BONE HEALTH FOCUS)

---

1. OVERALL DESIGN DIRECTION

The dashboard should feel:

* Calm
* Premium
* Minimal
* Intelligent

Avoid:

* Clinical layouts
* Data overload
* Harsh visuals

Use:

* Soft gradients (dark blue → light blue)
* Orange accents for key highlights
* Card-based layout with spacing and depth

---

2. HERO SECTION (TOP OF DASHBOARD)

Create a visually strong “at a glance” section:

Include:

* Welcome message (personalised)
* “Today’s Focus” (2–3 simple actions)
* Subtle status indicator (e.g. “Building consistency”)

Optional:

* Light animated background (very subtle, calming)

---

3. BONE HEALTH SUMMARY CARD (KEY FEATURE)

Create a premium summary card displaying:

* Latest DEXA status

* Fracture risk:

  * Major Osteoporotic (%)
  * Hip Fracture (%)

* Simple status:

  * Improving / Stable / Needs attention

Design:

* Rounded card
* Soft shadow
* Clean typography
* Clear hierarchy

---

4. PROGRESS VISUALISATION (HIGH-END)

Replace basic charts with elegant visuals:

* Smooth line graph (minimal gridlines)
* Soft gradient fill under the line
* Toggle between:

  * Bone density
  * Fracture risk
  * BMI

Include:

* Time filters (e.g. last 3, 6, 12 months)

Keep:

* Clean
* Easy to read
* Visually calming

---

5. CONSISTENCY & HABITS SECTION

Create a visual indicator of engagement:

* Weekly consistency (e.g. dots, rings, or bars)
* Categories:

  * Movement
  * Nutrition
  * Mindfulness

Design should feel:

* Light
* Encouraging
* Not overly gamified

---

6. “FEEL GOOD” INSIGHT CARD (DIFFERENTIATOR)

Add a dedicated insight section:

Examples:

* “You’ve been feeling calmer this week”
* “Your consistency is supporting your bone health”

Design:

* Soft background
* Minimal text
* Calm tone

This is emotional reinforcement, not data.

---

7. SNAP SHOT (WEEKLY SUMMARY PREVIEW)

Display a preview card:

* Weekly progress summary
* Positive reinforcement

CTA:
“View full SNAP Shot”

---

8. QUICK ACTIONS

Add simple, accessible buttons:

* Log DEXA
* Calculate FRAX
* Start Breathing Session
* View Meal Plan

Keep:

* Minimal
* Clearly spaced
* Not cluttered

---

9. MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Add subtle animations:

* Smooth transitions between states
* Soft hover/tap feedback
* Gentle graph animations

Keep all motion:

* Slow
* Premium
* Non-distracting

---

10. DATA HIERARCHY

Ensure:

* Most important info is visible first
* Secondary data is accessible but not overwhelming
* No duplication of information

---

11. MOBILE-FIRST DESIGN

Ensure dashboard works seamlessly on mobile:

* Scrollable sections
* Easy tap targets
* Clean stacking of cards

---

12. PERFORMANCE

* Fast load times
* Smooth rendering of graphs
* No lag between sections

---

GOAL

Deliver a dashboard that:

* Feels premium and high-end
* Makes data easy to understand
* Reinforces progress and motivation
* Encourages daily engagement

Users should feel:

* Calm
* In control
* Motivated

This should feel like a luxury wellness experience — not a clinical tool or basic app.
