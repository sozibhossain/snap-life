Update the Nutrition Log experience to align with UK clinical guidance and improve accuracy, understanding, and confidence for users managing osteoporosis and bone health.

The Nutrition Log should reflect:

* NICE osteoporosis guidance
* MHRA supplement and safety guidance
* UK NHS nutritional recommendations for bone health

The experience should help users understand:

* what nutrients matter for osteoporosis
* safe and effective intake ranges
* how to accurately log supplements and foods
* what measurements mean (mg, mcg, IU, g)
* how to avoid under- or over-supplementation

The tone should feel:

* clinically trustworthy
* NHS-aligned
* supportive and easy to understand
* designed for older adults and non-technical users

2. IMPROVE UNIT EDUCATION (mg / mcg / IU / g)

---

Add plain-English explanations beside all nutrient entries.

Examples:

* mg = milligrams
* mcg = micrograms
* g = grams
* IU = International Units

Provide contextual osteoporosis examples:

“Vitamin D supplements are often shown in IU or micrograms.”
“1000 IU Vitamin D is equal to 25 micrograms.”

“Calcium supplements are usually measured in milligrams (mg).”

Include tap-to-expand educational tooltips.

3. ADD SAFE RANGE + WARNING GUIDANCE

---

Add clinically appropriate validation and educational warnings.

Examples:

* Warn if calcium intake appears excessively high
* Warn against excessive vitamin D supplementation without clinical supervision
* Encourage discussing supplements with GP/pharmacist if values exceed guidance

Example microcopy:
“Taking more is not always better. Very high calcium or vitamin D intake may not be safe without medical advice.”

Do NOT present medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations.

Use supportive language:
“If unsure, speak with your healthcare professional.”
