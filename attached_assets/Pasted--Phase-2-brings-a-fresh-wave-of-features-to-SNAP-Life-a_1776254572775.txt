**Phase 2** brings a fresh wave of features to SNAP Life, all designed to make bone health more fun, varied, and motivating—especially for people living with Osteoporosis and Osteopenia. Our new theme, **Fuel • Calm • Celebrate**, captures our mission to support users with nourishing food, calming routines, and daily encouragement.

### What’s New in Phase 2?

#### 1. **Fuel for Bones: Smarter Nutrition & Meal Planning**

*   **AI-powered meal planning**: Users can now get personalised weekly meal plans and shopping lists, tailored to their dietary needs and preferences.
*   **Quick nutrition tips**: “SNAP Shot” micro-content delivers bite-sized, bone-healthy food ideas and facts.
*   **Bone Buddy Nutrition Coach**: Our AI assistant now gives friendly, evidence-based advice on calcium, vitamin D, and protein—making healthy eating simple and achievable.

#### 2. **Calm: Meditation & Breathing Variety**

*   **Chilled music for meditation**: Enjoy relaxing soundscapes during mindfulness sessions, with options to customise your experience.
*   **Breathing Studio**: Try a variety of guided breathing techniques (like box breathing, 4-7-8, diaphragmatic, and SNAP’s own 4-4-6 pattern), each matched to different moments—whether you need energy, calm, or a quick reset.
*   **Streaks and badges**: Stay motivated with new rewards for meditation and breathwork consistency.

#### 3. **Celebrate: Supportive Notifications & Micro-Wins**

*   **Encouraging notifications**: Receive positive, personalised nudges that celebrate your progress, remind you to care for your bones, and offer timely tips—always in a supportive, non-judgemental tone.
*   **Micro-wins and challenges**: Unlock new badges, complete fun quests (like “Calcium Quest” or “Mindful Minutes”), and celebrate every small step towards stronger bones.

**Why it matters:**  
Phase 2 makes SNAP Life more engaging and supportive, helping users build healthy habits with variety, encouragement, and a sense of achievement—every single day.
