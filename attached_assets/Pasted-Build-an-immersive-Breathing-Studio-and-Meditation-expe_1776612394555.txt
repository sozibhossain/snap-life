Build an immersive “Breathing Studio” and “Meditation” experience within the Snap Life app that goes beyond functionality and creates a daily habit loop focused on how users feel.

The goal is to make this a core daily ritual — not just for bone health, but for emotional wellbeing, stress reduction, and consistency.

---

BREATHING STUDIO REQUIREMENTS:

1. Sessions:
Create 4 guided breathing sessions:
- Calm (slow breathing)
- Energy (uplifting rhythm)
- Focus (balanced breathing)
- Sleep (deep, slow relaxation)

2. Timer + Visual Guidance:
- Each session must include a clear, easy-to-read timer
- Add a breathing animation (expanding/contracting shape or orb)
- Sync animation precisely with inhale / hold / exhale phases
- Include subtle prompts: “Inhale”, “Hold”, “Exhale”

3. Relaxing Background Music:
- Automatically play soft, calming background audio when session starts
- Use ambient or nature-based sounds
- Music must:
  - Loop seamlessly
  - Be gentle and non-distracting
  - Fade in at start and fade out at end
- Include:
  - Sound ON/OFF toggle
  - Volume control (default low)

4. User Experience:
- Minimal, calming interface
- Soft colours (blues, greens, warm neutrals)
- Smooth transitions (no abrupt changes)
- Designed for low cognitive effort

5. Completion + Reflection (CRITICAL – HABIT LOOP):
At the end of each session:
- Display a gentle message:
  “Well done. Take a moment to notice how you feel.”

- Prompt user with:
  “How do you feel now?”

Options:
- Calm
- Energised
- Less stressed
- Focused
- Still tense

- Store this response as part of the user’s daily wellbeing log

- Reinforce habit:
  “You’re building a daily habit for a stronger body and calmer mind.”

- Show streak indicator (e.g. “Day 3 in a row”)

---

MEDITATION SECTION REQUIREMENTS:

1. Session Types:
Create guided sessions for:
- Stress relief
- Sleep support
- Focus & clarity
- Confidence / mindset

2. Voice Guidance:
- Include a calm, human-like voiceover
- Tone must be:
  - Warm
  - Reassuring
  - Slow and well-paced
- Include natural pauses for breathing and reflection
- Avoid robotic or rushed delivery

3. Audio Design:
- Combine voice with soft background music
- Ensure voice is always clear and dominant
- Smooth fade in/out

4. Playback Controls:
- Play / pause
- Progress bar
- Restart
- Skip forward/back (10 seconds)

5. Screen Design:
- Minimal UI
- Optional subtle animation (slow gradient, breathing orb)
- Clear session title and duration

---

DAILY HABIT SYSTEM (KEY DIFFERENTIATOR):

- Track completion of breathing/meditation sessions daily
- Log how the user feels after each session
- Build a “Daily Wellbeing Score” based on:
  - Session completed
  - Emotional feedback
  - Consistency (streaks)

- Display:
  - Daily streaks
  - Weekly progress
  - Positive reinforcement messages

Example:
“You’ve completed 4 sessions this week — your consistency is building resilience.”

---

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:

- Ensure seamless audio playback (no glitches)
- Preload audio for smooth experience
- Optimise for mobile
- Keep assets lightweight for fast loading
- Ensure sessions run reliably on slower connections

---

GOAL:

Create a calm, premium, and emotionally engaging experience that users want to return to daily.

This is not just a feature — it is a daily ritual that connects physical health (bone strength) with mental wellbeing and emotional awareness.
