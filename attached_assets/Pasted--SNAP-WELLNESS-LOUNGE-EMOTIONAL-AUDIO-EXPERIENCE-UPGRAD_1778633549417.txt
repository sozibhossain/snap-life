🧘‍♀️ SNAP WELLNESS LOUNGE — EMOTIONAL AUDIO EXPERIENCE UPGRADE
Core design principle
Each session should feel like:
“A gentle guided story that meets the user exactly where they are emotionally, and brings them somewhere safer, steadier, or more uplifted.”
Not instruction. Not meditation coaching.
But:
emotional companionship through narrative
Tone:
calm British female voice
warm, grounded, reassuring
emotionally aware but not overly “therapy-like”
subtle, present, human
🌿 1. STRESS RELIEF (5 mins)
Emotional intention:
Overwhelm → grounding → safety → control returning
Narrative structure:
You open with recognition:
“You don’t need to fix everything right now.”
Then validation:
“There is a lot you may be carrying today… and it makes sense that it feels heavy.”
Then grounding:
“For the next few minutes, nothing is required of you.”
Then body safety cue:
“Allow your shoulders to soften… just slightly… as if you are setting something down.”
Then nervous system shift:
“Right now, you are safe enough to pause.”
Then gentle reframing:
“You are not behind. You are not failing. You are simply here… and that is enough for this moment.”
Close:
“When you are ready, the world will still be there… but you may feel a little more steady within it.”
🌙 2. SLEEP SUPPORT (7 mins)
Emotional intention:
Mental noise → soft landing → surrender → rest
Narrative structure:
Opening:
“You do not need to solve anything tonight.”
Soft deceleration:
“Let your thoughts slow… as if they are gently stepping further away from you.”
Body relaxation:
“Your jaw can unclench… your tongue can rest… your body can begin to soften its edges.”
Memory release:
“Anything unfinished can wait until tomorrow. Nothing needs to be carried into sleep.”
Emotional safety:
“There is nothing you need to do to deserve rest.”
Transition:
“Sleep is not something you force… it is something you allow.”
Close:
“You are safe to drift now… and tomorrow will meet you gently when it comes.”
🎯 3. FOCUS & CLARITY (6 mins)
Emotional intention:
Mental fog → structure → light clarity → forward motion
Narrative structure:
Opening:
“You don’t need more thoughts… you just need space between them.”
Reframing:
“Clarity is not about thinking harder… it is about thinking less, but more clearly.”
Grounding:
“Bring your attention gently to one small thing… just one.”
Control return:
“You are allowed to focus on only what matters in this moment.”
Flow state:
“Let everything else soften into the background.”
Confidence cue:
“You can take this one step at a time… and that is enough.”
Close:
“Clarity often arrives quietly… when we stop chasing it.”
💛 4. CONFIDENCE & MINDSET (6 mins)
Emotional intention:
Self-doubt → self-trust → grounded confidence → identity strengthening
Narrative structure:
Opening:
“You are not starting from nothing… you are starting from experience.”
Reframing doubt:
“Doubt does not mean you are incapable… it simply means you care.”
Inner strength:
“There is a steadiness within you that does not disappear, even on difficult days.”
Identity shift:
“You are someone who continues… even when things feel uncertain.”
Empowerment:
“Confidence is not loud. It is steady. Quiet. Consistent.”
Forward movement:
“You don’t need to feel ready. You just need to begin.”
Close:
“Each time you show up for yourself… you strengthen your sense of self.”
🌱 NEW MEDITATION / AUDIO GROUPS YOU SHOULD ADD
Now that your system is expanding into emotional wellbeing, there are 3 high-value missing categories that will significantly improve retention and emotional coverage:
🌤 5. CALM / REGULATION (NEW CORE CATEGORY)
Purpose:
Baseline nervous system balance (not problem-solving, just stabilising)
Use when:
users feel “fine but unsettled”
daily grounding practice
Narrative tone:
“You don’t need a reason to slow down.”
Content themes:
gentle breathing
emotional neutrality
presence
stability
🌧 6. SADNESS / WORRY SUPPORT (HIGH VALUE ADD)
Purpose:
Emotional holding space (not fixing, just supporting)
Very important for osteoporosis + menopause users where anxiety can be high.
Narrative structure:
Opening:
“If things feel heavy today… you don’t have to carry that alone in this moment.”
Validation:
“Sadness and worry are not problems to solve quickly… they are experiences that deserve gentleness.”
Permission:
“You are allowed to feel exactly as you feel right now.”
Soft grounding:
“Let your body be supported… even if your thoughts are unsettled.”
Reassurance:
“This feeling is temporary… even if it feels strong right now.”
Close:
“You are not required to change how you feel… only to be with yourself kindly while you feel it.”
☀️ 7. HAPPINESS / POSITIVITY (NEW UPLIFT CATEGORY)
Purpose:
Light emotional elevation + gratitude + energy lift
Not hype. Not excitement. More:
gentle optimism
Narrative:
Opening:
“There are moments, even small ones, where things feel a little lighter.”
Attention shift:
“Let your mind notice one thing that feels okay right now.”
Expansion:
“Not everything needs to be solved for you to experience a moment of ease.”
Warmth:
“You are allowed to feel good without needing a reason.”
Grounded joy:
“This feeling does not need to be chased… just noticed.”
Close:
“You can carry this small lightness with you into the rest of your day.”
