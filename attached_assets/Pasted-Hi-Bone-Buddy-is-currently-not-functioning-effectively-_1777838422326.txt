Hi — Bone Buddy is currently not functioning effectively in the development environment.

We need to rebuild and enhance this feature so it becomes a reliable, intelligent, and connected guidance system within SNAP Life.

This is not just a chatbot — it must be fully integrated with user data and the dashboard to provide meaningful, personalised support.

---

BONE BUDDY – CORE REBUILD & ENHANCEMENT

---

1. CORE OBJECTIVE

Build Bone Buddy as a:

* Personalised guidance assistant
* Data-aware insight engine
* Supportive, conversational interface

It must:

* Understand user data
* Reference real activity
* Provide helpful, non-clinical guidance

---

2. DATA CONNECTION (CRITICAL)

Bone Buddy must connect to and read:

* Bone Tracker (DEXA + FRAX + risk)
* Activity (movement sessions)
* Breathing sessions
* Meditation sessions
* Nutrition / meal tracking
* Mood / check-ins
* SNAP Shot summaries

No generic responses — all outputs must reflect real user data.

---

3. PERSONALISED CONVERSATION STYLE

Responses must:

* Use the user’s name
* Feel conversational (not robotic)
* Be concise (no long paragraphs)
* Ask relevant follow-up questions

Remove:

* Symbols like # or *
* Overly structured or scripted responses

---

4. GUIDANCE RULES (IMPORTANT)

Bone Buddy must:

* Provide general wellness guidance only
* NOT provide medical diagnosis or treatment

Always include when relevant:

* Suggestion to consult:

  * GP / Doctor
  * Specialist (e.g. endocrinologist, physiotherapist, nutritionist)

Example:
“If you’re unsure about these results, it would be a good idea to speak with your GP or a bone health specialist.”

---

5. WEEKLY CHECK-IN SYSTEM

Introduce structured weekly check-ins:

Bone Buddy should:

* Prompt user once per week
* Ask simple questions:

  * How are you feeling this week?
  * Energy levels?
  * Consistency with habits?

Capture responses and store them.

---

6. INSIGHT GENERATION

Based on data, generate insights such as:

* “You’ve been consistent with your breathing sessions this week”
* “Your activity levels have increased — great progress”
* “Your bone health data suggests it’s worth reviewing with a specialist”

Keep:

* Supportive
* Clear
* Easy to understand

---

7. DAILY SUPPORT (LIGHT TOUCH)

Provide optional daily interaction:

* “Today’s focus” reinforcement
* Gentle nudges (not intrusive)

---

8. CONTEXT AWARENESS

Ensure responses consider:

* Recent activity (last 7 days)
* Long-term trends (if available)
* Bone health status (DEXA + FRAX)

---

9. ESCALATION LOGIC

Introduce simple rules:

If:

* High fracture risk
* Osteoporosis classification
* Declining trends

Then:

* Encourage user to seek professional advice

Keep tone:

* Calm
* Supportive
* Not alarming

---

10. MEMORY & LEARNING

Ensure system:

* Stores past interactions
* Learns patterns over time
* Improves relevance of suggestions

---

11. UI / UX EXPERIENCE

* Chat-style interface
* Clean, minimal design
* Easy input and response flow

Optional:

* Quick reply buttons for common responses

---

12. PERFORMANCE

* Fast response time
* No lag
* Reliable output

---

GOAL

Build Bone Buddy as a core intelligence layer that:

* Connects all user data
* Provides meaningful, personalised guidance
* Encourages consistency and engagement
* Supports users without crossing into medical advice

This should feel:

* Human
* Helpful
* Intelligent
* Trustworthy
