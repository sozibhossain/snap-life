Enhance SNAP Life into a fully connected, intelligent health platform where all features work together to guide the user through simple, daily actions that improve bone health and overall wellbeing.

The system must feel personal, effortless, and supportive — not complex or overwhelming.

---

CORE PRINCIPLE:

SNAP Life should act as a “daily guide” that answers:

“What should I do today to support my health?”

All features must connect and contribute to this answer.

---

CORE SYSTEM COMPONENTS (CONNECTED):

Integrate and connect the following:

- Breathing Studio
- Meditation
- Meal Plans
- Bone Buddy (AI)
- Daily Check-In (mood)
- SNAP Shot (weekly reflection)
- Bone Health Data (FRAX, assessments)
- Subscription tier (Plus / Premium)

All data flows into a central intelligence layer.

---

1. DAILY “WHAT TO DO TODAY” ENGINE:

Create a simple daily plan shown on the home screen:

Example:
“Today’s focus”

- One nutrition goal (e.g. calcium-rich meal)
- One mental wellbeing action (breathing/meditation)
- One lifestyle habit (movement, sunlight, etc.)

Keep to 2–3 actions max.

This must be:
- Personalised
- Simple
- Actionable

---

2. USER “DIGITAL PROFILE” (LIGHT DIGITAL TWIN):

Use:
- FRAX score
- Behaviour (sessions, meals, mood)
- Engagement patterns

To generate simple insights:

Examples:
- “You’re building consistency this week”
- “Your current habits are supporting bone strength”

Avoid technical language.

---

3. NERVOUS SYSTEM STATUS:

Based on:
- Mood check-ins
- App usage (breathing/meditation)

Display simple state:
- Calm
- Balanced
- Stressed

Use this to recommend:
- Breathing sessions
- Meditation type
- Daily focus

---

4. CONNECTED INSIGHTS ENGINE:

Translate behaviour into meaning:

Examples:
- “You’ve felt calmer after evening sessions”
- “Your consistency with meals is supporting your bone health”

Keep insights:
- Short
- Encouraging
- Easy to understand

---

5. BONE BUDDY (CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE):

Bone Buddy must:

- Use user name
- Reference real data
- Speak conversationally

Responsibilities:
- Daily check-ins
- Recommend actions
- Reinforce progress
- Connect all features

Example:
“Hi Catherine, I can see you’ve been consistent this week. Would you like to focus on a calcium-rich meal today?”

---

6. FEATURE CONNECTIONS:

Ensure features influence each other:

- Breathing → affects mood → influences recommendations
- Meal Plans → supports bone health insights
- Mood → influences daily plan
- Activity → feeds SNAP Shot

No feature should exist in isolation.

---

7. MOVEMENT INTEGRATION (NEW):

Add simple bone-strength supporting movement:

- Light strength exercises
- Impact-safe guidance

Position as:
“Support your bones through movement”

Keep:
- Simple
- Beginner-friendly

---

8. SMART FOOD GUIDANCE (ENHANCED):

Beyond meal plans, add:

“What can I eat today?”

- Quick suggestions:
  - At home
  - On the go
  - Eating out

Keep simple and realistic.

---

9. SNAP SHOT (WEEKLY LOOP):

Provide:

- Weekly summary
- Emotional insight
- Reinforcement of consistency

Example:
“You’ve shown great consistency this week”

Points run in background (for future rewards).

---

10. PROGRESS WITHOUT COMPLEX DATA:

Avoid charts and overwhelming dashboards.

Instead show:
- “You’re improving”
- “You’re building momentum”

Focus on:
- Feeling
- Behaviour
- consistency

---

11. IDENTITY & HABIT BUILDING:

Reinforce identity:

- “You’re consistent”
- “You’re building strength”

Use light streaks (no pressure).

---

12. PREMIUM DIFFERENTIATION:

Premium unlocks:

- Advanced insights
- Adaptive recommendations
- Deeper Bone Buddy intelligence
- Full personalisation

Plus remains:
- Functional
- Supportive
- Less adaptive

---

13. CONTINUOUS LEARNING SYSTEM:

The app must learn from:

- User behaviour
- Mood trends
- Engagement patterns

Then:
- Adjust recommendations
- Refine tone
- Improve guidance

---

14. USER EXPERIENCE:

- Clean, calm interface
- Minimal inputs required
- No overwhelm

Everything should feel:
- Guided
- Personal
- Easy

---

GOAL:

Transform SNAP Life into an intelligent daily health companion that:

- Understands the user
- Guides simple actions
- Connects all aspects of health
- Improves bone health over time

Users should feel:
“I know what to do”
“I feel better”
“I’m making progress”

This is not just an app — it is a connected health system.
