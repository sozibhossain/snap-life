Enhance the Guided Meditation section in SNAP Life by implementing a premium, session-length-matched audio experience that complements the voiceover.

The music must feel intentional, immersive, and supportive — never distracting.

---

CORE PRINCIPLE:

The voiceover is the primary guide.
The music exists to:
- Support emotion
- Create atmosphere
- Maintain flow

It must never compete with or overpower the voice.

---

MUSIC STYLE (OVERALL DIRECTION):

Use a chilled, premium sound inspired by:
- Ibiza sunset chill
- Ambient soul
- Soft lounge / downtempo
- Light jazz textures (very subtle)
- Warm, organic tones

Avoid:
- Harsh electronic sounds
- Fast beats
- Busy arrangements
- Anything that distracts attention

---

SESSION-LENGTH MATCHING (CRITICAL):

Each meditation must have music that:

- Matches the full duration of the session (e.g. 5, 10, 15 minutes)
- Does NOT feel like a short loop repeating
- Evolves subtly over time

Structure the music in 3 phases:

1. Opening (first 10–15%):
- Very soft, minimal layers
- Gentle fade in
- Creates sense of arrival

2. Middle (main body):
- Stable, consistent soundscape
- Slight evolution (layering, subtle variation)
- Supports breathing and focus

3. Closing (last 10–15%):
- Gradual reduction in layers
- Softer, slower feel
- Gentle fade out to silence

---

AUDIO BALANCE:

- Voice must always be clear and dominant
- Music volume should sit underneath (approx. 15–30% of voice level)
- Use soft EQ to avoid frequency clashes with voice

---

MOOD ALIGNMENT BY SESSION TYPE:

1. Stress Relief:
- Warm, reassuring tones
- Slow, gentle rhythm (if any)
- Feels safe and grounding

2. Sleep Support:
- Deep ambient textures
- No rhythm or beat
- Very slow, almost hypnotic

3. Focus & Clarity:
- Clean, minimal sound
- Light tonal movement
- Slight brightness (but soft)

4. Confidence / Mindset:
- Slightly uplifting tone
- Warm bass, gentle progression
- Positive but calm

---

TRANSITIONS:

- Fade music in at session start (1–2 seconds)
- Fade out naturally at end
- Avoid sudden stops or resets

---

PLAYBACK EXPERIENCE:

- Ensure seamless playback (no glitches)
- Preload audio for smooth start
- Sync music timing with voiceover duration

---

USER CONTROLS:

- Volume control (music independent if possible)
- Option to turn music OFF (voice only)

---

GOAL:

Create a guided meditation experience that feels like a continuous, immersive journey.

The user should not notice the music technically — but should feel:
- Supported
- Calm
- Engaged
- Present

The experience should feel premium, emotionally aligned, and worth returning to daily.
